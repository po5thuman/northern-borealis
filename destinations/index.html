Europe has some of the best places in the world to see the Northern Lights, but choosing where to go can completely change the experience. Norway combines Arctic cities with dramatic fjords, Iceland pairs the aurora with volcanic landscapes, Sweden offers remote wilderness in Swedish Lapland, and Finland combines dark Arctic skies with snowy forests, Rovaniemi, Levi and the wider Finnish Lapland experience.

We research and compare Northern Lights destinations across Europe so you can choose the country that best suits your trip before looking at individual tours. How to Choose a Northern Lights Destination

The best destination depends on the kind of Arctic trip you want. Norway is a strong choice for travellers who want to combine aurora chasing with fjords and Arctic coastal scenery. Iceland makes it easy to combine the Northern Lights with waterfalls, glaciers, geothermal landscapes and road trips. Swedish Lapland offers remote Arctic wilderness around Abisko and Kiruna, while Finnish Lapland combines aurora viewing with classic winter experiences such as reindeer, huskies, snowmobiling and Santa Claus Village.

Your travel dates matter too. Aurora visibility requires sufficiently dark skies, suitable weather and solar activity, so there is no destination where a sighting can be guaranteed simply by travelling there. If your dates are flexible, use our best time to see the Northern Lights guide to compare the season month by month.
