How to Read the Northern Lights Forecast

The Kp index is a planetary measure of geomagnetic activity. It runs from 0 to 9. Higher values generally indicate stronger geomagnetic activity and the potential for the auroral oval to expand farther from the polar regions.

For trip planning, the three-hour forecast is more useful than looking only at a single daily maximum. A day that reaches Kp 4 for one forecast period is not the same as a night that remains near Kp 4 for many hours. That is why the dashboard now shows the individual NOAA forecast periods as well as the daily peak.

The NOAA view-line maps estimate how far equatorward aurora may potentially be visible under favourable conditions. Treat them as guidance rather than a hard boundary: visibility still depends on darkness, cloud, local light pollution, aurora brightness and your view toward the relevant horizon.

What Is a Good Kp Index for the Aurora?

There is no single Kp number that guarantees a sighting. Your latitude matters enormously. In destinations beneath or close to the auroral oval — including Tromsø, Abisko and Finnish Lapland — aurora can appear during relatively low Kp activity.

Farther south, stronger geomagnetic activity is usually required before the auroral oval expands far enough for potential visibility. Kp 5 marks the beginning of NOAA's G1 geomagnetic-storm scale; Kp 6 corresponds to G2, Kp 7 to G3, Kp 8 to G4 and Kp 9 to G5.

⚡ The Golden Rule of Aurora Hunting: Kp tells you about geomagnetic activity — not whether the sky above you is clear. A lower-Kp night with clear skies in a strong Arctic location can be far more useful than a major geomagnetic event hidden behind cloud. Always check a reliable local cloud forecast alongside this page. See our full guide on how to see the Northern Lights.

What Causes the Aurora Borealis Forecast to Change?

The Northern Lights are produced when energetic particles associated with the solar wind interact with Earth's magnetic environment and upper atmosphere. Changes in solar-wind conditions and events such as coronal mass ejections can increase geomagnetic activity when their effects reach Earth.

NOAA's Space Weather Prediction Center monitors the Sun and near-Earth space environment and continually updates its forecasts as new observations become available. That is why short-range forecasts can change. If you want the science in more detail, read our guide to what the Northern Lights are.

Combining the Forecast with the Best Time to Travel

Even strong aurora activity cannot help if the sky is too bright to see it. Across much of the Arctic, the practical Northern Lights travel season runs from late summer or early autumn into early spring, when sufficiently dark nights return.

For a full month-by-month breakdown, see our guide to the best time to see the Northern Lights. If you are still choosing where to travel, compare the best places to see the Northern Lights.

Northern Lights Forecast FAQ

🌌 What is the Northern Lights forecast tonight?
The live dashboard at the top of this page uses NOAA Space Weather Prediction Center data to show predicted Kp activity in three-hour periods and the broader three-day outlook. Remember that Kp is only one part of aurora viewing: local cloud cover, darkness and location also matter.
📊 What is a good Kp index to see the aurora borealis?
It depends strongly on location. Destinations beneath or close to the auroral oval can see aurora during relatively low Kp activity, while locations farther south generally need stronger geomagnetic activity.
🎯 How accurate is a 3-day aurora forecast?
It is useful for understanding the broad geomagnetic outlook, but it cannot guarantee a local sighting. Space-weather conditions can change and local cloud, darkness and the position of the auroral oval remain critical.
☁️ Does a high Kp guarantee Northern Lights?
No. Kp measures global geomagnetic activity and does not account for local cloud cover. Even during strong activity you may see nothing beneath overcast skies, while Arctic destinations can sometimes see excellent aurora at comparatively low Kp values.
