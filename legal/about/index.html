About Northern Borealis

Independent Northern Lights travel guidance for comparing destinations, researching tours and planning unforgettable aurora trips across Europe.

Our Story

Making Aurora Trip Planning Simpler

Northern Borealis grew from a simple problem: planning a Northern Lights trip can quickly become confusing. There are hundreds of tours, changing weather conditions, unfamiliar Arctic destinations and plenty of advice that makes seeing the aurora sound either guaranteed or impossibly complicated.

We wanted to build something more useful — a focused travel resource where people can compare destinations, understand what matters when choosing a tour and learn how factors such as season, darkness, cloud cover and geomagnetic activity fit together.

Today, Northern Borealis covers aurora travel across Norway, Iceland, Finland and Sweden, combining destination guides, tour research and practical planning resources in one place.

✦
The Northern Lights are unpredictable. Planning your trip doesn't have to be.

Our aim is to make the decisions around where to go, when to travel and what to book clearer — without pretending anyone can guarantee what the sky will do.

Our Method

How We Research & Compare Tours

There is no single perfect Northern Lights tour for everyone. We look at the practical details that help distinguish one experience from another.

★
Traveller FeedbackRatings, review volume and recurring traveller experiences.
👥
Group SizePrivate, small-group and larger coach experiences suit different travellers.
✓
InclusionsTransport, guides, photography, clothing, refreshments and other extras.
↻
Booking PoliciesCancellation terms, rebooking options and any stated aurora policies.
€
Overall ValueWhat the experience offers relative to its price and intended traveller.

We use these factors to explain the strengths and trade-offs of different tours rather than assuming that the cheapest, most expensive or highest-rated option is automatically right for everyone.

Our Approach

Independent, Practical & Transparent

We want readers to understand both how we approach our travel content and how Northern Borealis is funded.

01 · INDEPENDENT

Recommendations Come First

Our goal is to highlight useful options based on the experience, traveller needs and available information — not simply because a particular booking link may earn a commission.

02 · PRACTICAL

No Aurora Guarantees

The Northern Lights are a natural phenomenon. We explain the conditions that influence visibility without presenting any forecast, destination or tour as a guaranteed sighting.

03 · TRANSPARENT

Clear About Commercial Links
