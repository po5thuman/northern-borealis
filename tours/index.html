How to Choose a Northern Lights Tour

Northern Lights tours can differ substantially even within the same destination. Before booking, look beyond the headline price and consider how long the tour lasts, how far the guide is prepared to travel, the group size and whether photography, winter clothing, food or hotel pickup are included.

Dedicated aurora chases generally devote most of the evening to finding suitable viewing conditions. These can be a good fit if seeing the Northern Lights is the main purpose of your trip.

Small-group and photography tours can provide a more personal experience and may include help photographing the aurora. Group limits vary considerably, so check the individual tour before booking.

Activity-based tours combine aurora watching with experiences such as snowmobiling, sledging or boat trips. They can be memorable, but the activity itself is often a larger part of the experience than actively chasing clear skies.

Whichever style you choose, remember that the Northern Lights are a natural phenomenon. Cloud cover, solar activity and local weather all affect visibility, and no tour can control whether the aurora appears.
